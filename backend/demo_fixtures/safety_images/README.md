# PubChem Demo safety images

These PubChem-sourced hazard pictograms and NFPA 704 images are bundled for an offline, illustrative Demo fixture. Source: PubChem (NCBI/NLM), with CID mappings recorded in `manifest.json`.

PubChem attribution and legal review remain required before downstream redistribution. PubChem records and derived safety graphics may have record-specific terms; this notice is not a claim of unconditional reuse rights. The fixture is not a substitute for a current safety data sheet, regulatory classification, or laboratory safety instruction.
